Pantry is the Chrome extension that skips the story on recipe blogs and saves recipes straight to your real Chrome bookmarks. This update adds new features you asked for – scroll down to learn more.

What's new

Four things we built for you.

Focus mode

New

View the recipe without any of the distractions. One click hides everything around the recipe card so you can actually read what you came for.

Screenshot coming

Contextual ingredients

New

Ingredient quantities appear inline right inside the instructions. No more scrolling up and down to cross-reference amounts — everything you need is exactly where you need it.

Screenshot coming

Floating bookmark button

New

A small bookmark button now floats on the recipe page so you can save with one click, right where you are — no need to reach for the toolbar extension icon.

Screenshot coming

Recipe notes

New

Write your own notes directly on the recipe page — substitutions, tweaks, what you'd do differently. They auto-save and sync across your Chrome browsers, so they're right there the next time you make it.
