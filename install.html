Welcome to Pantry

Head to any recipe blog and Pantry jumps straight to the recipe — then helps you save it, annotate it, and much more.

No account needed Works immediately Packed with features

Everything you wish recipe blogs did themselves.

🔖

Yours forever

Recipes save to your real Chrome bookmarks — not a SaaS database. They sync to mobile, search from the omnibox, and stay with you even if you uninstall Pantry.

🥬

Skip the story

Auto-scrolls past the preamble straight to the recipe the moment the page loads. No hunting for the “Jump to Recipe” link.

✅

One-click bookmark

Saved into a tidy, alphabetically sorted “Recipes” folder. No manual filing, no mess — it stays organized for you.

🍅

Ingredient cheat sheet

Hover any ingredient in the instructions to see the full line — “2 tbsp olive oil, divided” — without scrolling back up to the list.

📝

Recipe notes

A side panel for substitutions, tweaks, and what you’d do differently next time. Auto-saves and syncs across your Chrome browsers.

🔗

Deep links

Bookmarks remember the recipe’s exact position on the page, so revisiting jumps you right back to the good part.
